IT Incident Response Procedure

Effective Date: April 1, 2026

Policy Owner: Information Security

Severity

Production incidents are categorized by business impact and urgency. Critical incidents require immediate notification of the on-call team.

Documentation

Responders document the timeline, affected services, mitigation steps, and follow-up actions.

Post Incident Review

A review identifies root causes and preventive improvements.

Access Classification

Confidential

Allowed Roles: it_admin, security_admin, manager
