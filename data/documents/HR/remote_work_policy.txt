Remote Work Policy
Effective Date: March 1, 2026
Policy Owner: Human Resources

Eligibility
Eligible employees may work remotely according to their team's approved schedule.

Security
Employees must maintain reliable connectivity and use company-approved devices and authentication methods when accessing company resources.

Management
Managers are responsible for confirming schedules and ensuring required business coverage.

Access Classification
Internal

Allowed Roles: employee, manager, hr_admin
